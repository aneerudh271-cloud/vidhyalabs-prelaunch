Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

What we collect

  • Name, phone number and area when you join the early list.
  • Delivery address, order details and payment method when you place an order.
  • On your device only: your cart, order history and saved profile in the VidhyaLabs app.

Why we use it

To send launch updates and offers you asked for, to confirm and deliver your orders, and to give you support.

Who sees it

Our team and the delivery or service partners who fulfil your order. We never sell your data.

How long we keep it

Early-list details until you opt out or we finish launch communications. Order records for 1 year for accounting.

Your choices

You can withdraw consent, ask for a copy, correct or delete your data at any time. Message us in the app or call +91 74006 37753.

Terms of Service

Orders and delivery

Orders are placed through the app and confirmed by our team. Milk orders placed by the daily cut-off are delivered the next morning in the stated slot. Delivery is available only in areas we list.

Prices and payment

Milk and service prices are as shown. Prices for groceries, water, newspapers and vehicle cleaning are confirmed by our team before delivery. Pay by UPI or cash on delivery.

Subscriptions

You can pause, skip or cancel by messaging us before the daily cut-off. Changes after the cut-off apply from the next delivery.

Services

Home cleaning is charged per hour. Electrician, plumbing and appliance repair carry a ₹300 booking fee that is adjusted against the final bill. Milk, grocery and other delivery orders can be changed or cancelled in the app before 9pm the night before delivery. Service bookings can be rescheduled or cancelled without charge until the technician is on the way; after that the ₹300 booking fee may be kept.

Problems with an order

If an item is spoiled, wrong or missing, message us the same day and we will replace it or credit your account.

Questions, answered.

Which areas do you serve?

All of Bhopal. Choose your area in the app when you order. Indus Town, Krishnapuram and Radhapuram are our home neighbourhoods.

When is milk delivered?

Daily between 6–8am. Place or change your order by 9pm the night before.

How do subscriptions work?

Choose daily, weekly or monthly. Pause, skip or cancel any time by messaging us in the app before 9pm.

How do I pay?

UPI or cash on delivery.

What if my milk is spoiled or missing?

Message us the same day and we'll replace it or credit your account.

How does the ₹300 service booking fee work?

You pay it to book a technician, and it is deducted from your final bill.
